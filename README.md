# Clothesy

Clothesy is a wardrobe app. Photograph what you own, log what you wear, and plan laundry and trips. The goal is to get more wear out of the clothes you already have.

This repository only holds the app's downloads. More at **[clothesy.fyi](https://clothesy.fyi)**.

## Get the app

- **Android:** open the [latest release](https://github.com/jknysz/clothesy-releases/releases/latest) on your phone and tap **clothesy.apk**.
- **Web, iPhone and iPad:** use [app.clothesy.fyi](https://app.clothesy.fyi) in your browser. To keep it on your home screen, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.

### Installing the Android app

1. Download `clothesy.apk` from the latest release.
2. When your phone asks, allow your browser (or Files app) to install apps. Android asks this for any app that doesn't come from the Play Store.
3. Open the file and tap **Install**.

If the download stalls in Chrome, try it again in an Incognito tab.

Once it's installed, the app tells you when a new version is out, and **Update** installs it. Clothesy is coming to Google Play, which will skip the install warning.

## Releases and test builds

- **Releases** (`v1.3.0` and so on) are the regular app. Each release page lists what's new.
- **Clothesy test build** (the `preview` pre-release) is for testers. It installs as a separate app called *Clothesy Preview* and may include unfinished features.
